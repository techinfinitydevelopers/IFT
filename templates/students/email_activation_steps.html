🚀 IFT SCHOOL ACTIVATION JOURNEY

1. Popularise IFT in Your School

Use the posters, creatives, videos, and other marketing resources to promote IFT and encourage student registrations.

2. Drive Student Registrations

Track registrations and ensure all students complete payment to activate their IFT access.

3. Get Your Teacher Trained as an IFT Mentor

Encourage students to complete the prescribed learning videos and track progress on the dashboard.

4. Encourage Students to Go Through Learning Tutorials

Assigned IFT Teachers attend a Friday training session and implement the learning with students.

5. Mentor Student Ideas and Drive Idea Submissions

Conduct a school ideation workshop to help students turn their learning into innovative ideas.

6. IFTx at Your School

Give students a platform to present their ideas through a school-level IFTx showcase. Upload event photos, videos and a short summary on your School Dashboard.

We encourage you to follow these steps and actively drive participation within your school. Your support will help students make the most of their IFT journey!
