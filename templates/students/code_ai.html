menu_book About This Course

Dive into the exciting world of artificial intelligence with CodeAI's innovative module, "How AI Makes Decisions." This module is named to highlight its focus on unraveling the processes that AI systems use to analyze training data and make decisions. In this course, AI concepts are broken down into engaging lessons that focus on the critical role data plays in making AI work behind the scenes. Each lesson provides opportunities to learn and reflect about AI, bias, and the impact of AI tools on daily life.
