About IFT

India's Launchpad for
Young Entrepreneurs

Children are a powerhouse of ideas, yet often lack the right platforms to express creativity, solve real-world problems, and showcase their potential. India's Future Tycoons (IFT) bridges this gap for students aged 13–18 through mentorship, founder bootcamps, and a national pitch platform, enabling them to build solutions, gain innovation confidence, earn recognition, access funding, and unlock global opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

An exclusive entrepreneurship platform for schools, giving students the opportunity to showcase their ideas and gain national recognition. For students aged 13–18 years.
IFT is jointly organized by Tata ClassEdge and ENpower, a leading entrepreneurship education organization dedicated to nurturing entrepreneurial mindsets, innovation, and future-ready skills among school students across India.
Students aged 13–18 years from any of the Schools can register and submit their ideas, either individually or as teams. (1-2)
Yes, upon registration, you will be redirected towards a payment gateway.
The IFT journey has 8 stages from registration, learning, idea submission to several layers of idea evaluation like the TOP 400, TOP 100 & TOP 12. Students will experience online mentorship, brand workshops, expert masterclasses and a lot more. These opportunities will shape a student's idea and ultimately prepare them to face an esteemed jury. Students who make it to the winning stage will unlock a plethora of opportunities, rewards, and gifts that will mark the beginning of the journey to be India's Future Tycoons.
The TOP 12 ideas of the year are invited to a high-octane 2 to 3-day residential bootcamp where students get 1-on-1 mentorship from industry experts & entrepreneurs, they prepare for the grand finale pitch, and compete for prizes, recognition, and investor attention.
Every participant gets Digital Certificates of Participation. As the students progress to the TOP 12 - Hall Of Fame - the rewards get more & more exciting. Seed funding or international opportunities are just the beginning.
